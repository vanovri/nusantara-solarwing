01 / THE ENGINEERING PROBLEM

Endurance starts with an honest energy budget.

Solar cells can extend a daylight patrol. Cloud, propulsion load and battery capacity still set a firm limit. A 24-hour claim must close both the daily energy balance and the night storage requirement.

03 / SYSTEM PROPOSAL

Use energy to decide the route.

01

Estimate

Combine recent irradiance, battery SoC and mission power into a short-horizon energy forecast.

02

Constrain

Keep a 20% reserve. Account for return energy, forecast uncertainty and power needed for safe recovery.

03

Act

Patrol, shorten or return based on the energy margin. A human operator confirms mission changes.

Novelty to test

Energy-aware return planning already exists. This concept tests a Natuna-specific solar screen and conservative return rule against a fixed-duration patrol. Compare reserve violations, covered area and early returns on a hardware-in-the-loop bench.

04 / NATUNA MISSION GEOMETRY

A route with a return decision built in.

Ranai Airport is the geographic reference. The offshore search box and 20 km distance are planning assumptions, not an approved flight route.

FIVE-LEG CREEPING-LINE SEARCH

Five search legs total 60 km. Four lane changes add 8 km. With at least 20 km each way from the Ranai reference, 108 km is a lower-bound route length. Actual egress and turns can add distance. The 96 km² box is an assigned footprint, not confirmed camera coverage.

For that ideal 108 km route, zero solar and a 5 m/s return headwind, the modeled pack ends at 47.2% SoC. This excludes extra exit distance, turns and climb.

DECISION RULE / 20 KM EXAMPLE

How much battery must remain?

With a 5 m/s return headwind and no credit for future sunlight, return requires 101.5 Wh from the pack. Add 178.8 Wh reserve and an 89.4 Wh buffer. The next 12 km leg costs another 38.3 Wh. These are modeled battery debits with 95% discharge efficiency.

≤ 41.3% SoCReturn
41.3–45.6%Shorten
≥ 45.6% SoCPatrol

At every waypoint, maximize new search-line distance while keeping return energy, reserve and uncertainty buffer protected. Recompute from the actual position. A partial leg needs a safe exit.
